## Screenshot set

All six screens use real captures from the current Android build:

Use real captures from the current Android build. Do not fabricate controls or screens. Capture portrait screenshots at the device's native resolution, then export at 1080 x 1920 (9:16) as 24-bit PNG without alpha.

Recommended six-screen sequence:

1. Gallery categories: "Find your moment of calm"
2. Artwork selection: "A mandala for every mood"
3. Editor with a partly colored drawing: "Color with a tap"
4. Featured palette: "Make every shade your own"
5. Advanced color picker: "Your color, your way"
6. Share screen: "Save and share your calm"

Keep captions short, use the cream/turquoise/coral brand palette, and preserve a generous safe area around text. The first three screenshots should communicate gallery, coloring, and palette customization without requiring the user to read the description.

## Google Play constraints

- Icon: 512 x 512 PNG, maximum 1 MB.
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- Screenshots: JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; each side between 320 and 3840 px; the long side cannot exceed twice the short side.
- For recommendation-oriented phone media, prefer at least four 1080 x 1920 portrait screenshots.

## Brand direction

- Background: deep charcoal, subtly patterned.
- Primary: turquoise.
- Accents: coral-orange, raspberry, lime, and warm cream.
- Visual language: bold hand-inked mandala forms with fewer, larger shapes that remain readable at small sizes.
- Tone: calm and creative, not childish or clinical.
